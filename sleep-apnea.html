PRODUCT FEATURE

Explore VitalWeave

VitalWeave Is A Wearable Pulse Oximeter Designed For Continuous Monitoring Of SpO₂, Pulse Rate And Motion, With Support For Extended And Overnight Monitoring.

Continuous SpO₂

Track Blood Oxygen Levels Continuously For Clearer Long-Term Monitoring Insights.

Pulse Rate Tracking

Monitor Pulse-Rate Changes Continuously Alongside Oxygen Levels For Better Visibility.

Motion Monitoring

Capture Movement Information Alongside Vital Measurements Throughout The Monitoring Period.

Overnight Monitoring

Designed For Extended Monitoring, Including Comfortable All-Night Measurement During Sleep.

Smart Health Alerts

Receive Vibration Reminders When Configured Oxygen Or Pulse Thresholds Change.

Connected Data

Manage And Review Recorded Monitoring Data Through VitalWeave Cloud And Platforms.
