Overview

Overview of VitalWeave

Your Partner in Proactive Patient Care

VitalWeave is a compact, wearable device designed to monitor key vital parameters in real time. Developed with comfort, accuracy, and reliability in mind, it helps healthcare professionals observe patients' vital trends and respond at the right time. VitalWeave measures pulse rate, oxygen saturation (SpO2), blood pressure, and activity levels, providing continuous insights that support early detection and better clinical decision-making.
